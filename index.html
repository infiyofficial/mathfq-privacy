Privacy Policy

MathFQ  ·  Last updated: 2026

Summary: MathFQ does not collect, store, or sell any personal identification data. We use Meta Audience Network to serve banner and rewarded video ads. All game progress, settings, and coins are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

MathFQ does not require any account creation, registration, or personal login.

We do not directly collect or store:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • GPS precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, or personal device files

2. Data Stored on Your Device

MathFQ saves your gameplay progress locally using Android's private app storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Game scores, accuracy statistics, and streaks
  • Level unlock progress (earned by completing level tasks)
  • Active coins and time limit access (earned via rewarded video ads)
  • App theme preferences (Light / Dark mode)

This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our personal servers. Uninstalling the app permanently removes this local data.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

MathFQ integrates third-party advertising SDKs to serve banner ads and rewarded video ads:

  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook Ads): Used to deliver banner advertisements and rewarded video ads (which reward users with in-app coins for play time). Meta may automatically collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad performance data to deliver and measure ads. For more information, visit Meta's Privacy Policy and Meta Ad Preferences.
  • Google Play Services: Distributed via Google Play. Google collects download, installation, and crash statistics outside our direct control. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Network Permissions

MathFQ uses the following standard Android permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Required to load and display ads from Meta Audience Network and allow optional sharing/email support actions.

5. Children's Privacy

MathFQ is designed for general audiences aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

All user progress is stored locally on your device. You can erase all data at any time by:

  • Going to Android Settings → Apps → MathFQ → Storage → Clear Data
  • Or simply uninstalling the MathFQ app

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
