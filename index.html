Privacy Policy

MathFQ  ·  Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary: MathFQ does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline. All your data (scores, level progress, settings) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. MathFQ does not require any account, sign-in, or registration.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data (GPS or approximate)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID)
  • Contacts, photos, files, or media
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

2. Data Stored on Your Device

MathFQ saves the following information locally on your device using Android's private app storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Game scores (correct, incorrect, streaks)
  • Level progress (levels unlocked and cleared)
  • App settings (sound, vibration, theme preference)

This data is never transmitted to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

3. Internet Access

MathFQ does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not perform any network requests on its own.

The only network activity that can occur is when you tap:

  • Share App — opens your device's share sheet with a Play Store link.
  • Rate Us — opens the Google Play Store page for MathFQ.
  • Email Support — opens your preferred email app with our address pre-filled.

These actions hand control to another app; they are initiated only by your explicit tap.

4. Third-Party Services

MathFQ does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking libraries.

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google's own privacy policy applies to the Play Store's collection of download and install statistics, which is outside our control. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

MathFQ requests no runtime permissions and no special Android permissions. The app works entirely offline with default sandbox access.

6. Children's Privacy

MathFQ is designed for general audiences aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, there is nothing to delete upon request.

7. Data Deletion

Since MathFQ stores all data locally on your device, you can delete it at any time:

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → MathFQ → Storage → Clear data
  • Or simply uninstall the app

Both actions permanently remove all locally stored information. There is no server-side copy to delete.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
